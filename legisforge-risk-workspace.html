COR-014 High severity Ownership

PSC register omits beneficial owner above 25% threshold

Summary

The Persons with Significant Control register filed at Companies House lists two individuals holding voting rights, but the underlying Shareholders' Agreement identifies a third party, Halvard Reiss, holding a 31% indirect interest through a nominee arrangement disclosed at cl. 4.2 ↗. This party does not appear on the statutory register.

Analysis

Under the relevant PSC regime, any individual holding more than 25% of shares or voting rights — directly or through an intermediary — must be recorded. The nominee structure described in the agreement appears to bring Mr Reiss within scope. The omission may constitute:

  1. A failure to maintain an accurate PSC register under the Companies Act;
  2. A disclosure gap relevant to the buyer's ownership warranties;
  3. Grounds for a corrective filing prior to completion.

Recommendation

Request confirmation from the target's company secretary on the nominee arrangement, and require a corrective PSC filing as a condition to closing, or as a specific indemnity if timing does not allow.
