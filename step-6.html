Executive summary

This report summarises the findings of legal due diligence conducted across five departments in connection with the proposed acquisition of the target company. A total of 41 issues were identified across 58 reviewed documents, including 7 registry mismatches against Companies House and the relevant IP register.

The most material findings concern gaps in the target's PSC and charge filings (Corporate), an unresolved dual-purpose licensing arrangement (Corporate/IP), and an undisclosed related-party loan. None of the findings identified are considered transaction-blocking, but several are recommended as conditions precedent or specific indemnities, set out by department below.
